How to cook pizza

Description

A simple homemade pizza with a crisp crust, tangy tomato sauce, and melted mozzarella. Keep it classic, or throw on toppings you actually like.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Olive oil
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomate sauce and crushed tomatoes
  • Parmesan
  • Mozzarella
  • Herbs & spices: salt, pepper, Italian herbs, fresh basil, oregano
  • Toppings (optional): pepperoni, mushrooms, bell pepper, olives, etc.

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven as hot as it goes (230°-250°C). If you have a tray, heat it too.
  2. Stretch or roll the dough into a round (or whatever shape happens). Place it on a baking paper.
  3. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce over the rolled out dough, leaving a small border for the crust.
  4. Add mozzarella and your toppings of choice. Don't overload it or it goes soggy!
  5. Drizzle lightly with olive oil and sprinkle oregano, salt and pepper.
  6. Bake in the oven until the crust is browned and the cheese is bubbling (usually 8-15 minutes depending on how strong your oven is).
  7. Let it cool for a minute, slice, and enjoy!
