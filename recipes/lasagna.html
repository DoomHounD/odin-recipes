How to cook lasagna

Description

A classic, layered lasagna with rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta and a golden cheese crust. Comfort food that actually tastes better the next day.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef or sausage
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomate sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella
  • Parmesan
  • Herbs & spices: salt, pepper, Italian herbs

Steps

  1. Cook noodles and preheat oven to 180°C
  2. Brown your meat of choice and add onion & garlic, followed by adding tomato sauce and let it all simmer
  3. Layer your lasagna in the following order: start with sauce, then noodles, ricotta, and cheese. Repeat these layers
  4. Put the lasagna in the oven and bake until bubbly with a nice golden crisp top
