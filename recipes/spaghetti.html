How to cook spaghetti

Description

A simple, classic spaghetti with a rich tomato sauce, garlic and herbs. Quick enough for a weeknight, but it tastes like you actually tried.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti pasta
  • Olive oil
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomate sauce and crushed tomatoes
  • Parmesan
  • Herbs & spices: salt, pepper, Italian herbs, fresh basil

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti pasta until al dente/li>
  2. While the pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a pan and sauté onion until soft.
  3. Add garlic and cook briefly (don't burn it), then add tomato sauce and herbs.
  4. Simmer the sauce for 10-15 mintues. Taste and adjust with salt and pepper.
  5. Drain pasta (save a splash of pasta water). Toss spaghetti with sauce, loosening with pasta water if needed
  6. Serve with parmesan and fresh basil (if you want to be fancy, which we do).
